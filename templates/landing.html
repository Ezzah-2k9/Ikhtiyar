What can I compare with Ikhtiyar?

Compare driving, taxi, metro, bus, walking and cycling for estimated journey time, cost and carbon emissions.

Does Ikhtiyar learn from my trips?

Yes. As you record journeys, the app can build personalized transport recommendations based on your travel history.

Is Ikhtiyar made for travel in the UAE?

Ikhtiyar is designed around UAE travel, including local fare and cost estimates where available.
